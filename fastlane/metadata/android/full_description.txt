M64Plus Quest brings the Nintendo 64 into VR on your Meta Quest. It is a fork of the well known Mupen64Plus-AE Android emulator, rebuilt as a native OpenXR app: the game hangs on a screen you place in your room, or fills your whole view in immersive mode.

FEATURES
- Native VR, not a flat app in a window: a sharp OpenXR screen you grab and place yourself, with passthrough so your room stays visible around it
- Touch controllers act as a real N64 controller, mapped one to one, no remapping needed
- A 3D N64 controller model rendered in your hands, lighting up as you press buttons
- Experimental stereoscopic 3D and immersive mode: the screen disappears, the game renders at the headset's field of view, and turning your head turns the camera (see the project docs for the short list of tested games)
- Netplay from inside VR: host or join with a ten digit room code, playable together with Mupen64Plus-AE running on a phone
- RetroAchievements support, softcore, so save states stay available
- Everything else Mupen64Plus-AE offers: save states, texture packs, shaders, cheats

GETTING STARTED
1. Turn on developer mode on your Quest and connect it through SideQuest
2. Install this APK from SideQuest
3. Open the app, tap Add ROMs, and point it at a folder holding your own N64 game backups
4. Pick a game from your library and start playing
